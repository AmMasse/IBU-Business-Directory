We do not sell your personal information.

4. Data Protection

We implement industry-standard security measures, including:

  • Encryption in transit and at rest.
  • Restricted access to sensitive data.
  • Regular data audits and security reviews.

5. User Rights

As a user of our service, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Update or delete your business listing at any time using the in-app delete option.
  • Withdraw your consent to our processing of your data, where applicable.
  • Request deletion of your account by contacting us. Account deletions will be processed within 7 business days.

If you wish to exercise any of these rights, please contact us at ibudirectory@gmail.com.

6. Children’s Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we discover such data, we delete it immediately.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
